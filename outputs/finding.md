# Observed evidence snapshot

The full processed output contains 7,578 DDT-eligible snap-to-throw plays from 122 games. Geometric DDT appears on 5,473 plays (72.2%), with a mean duration of 0.65 seconds.

## Descriptive coverage contrast

Among coverage groups with at least 100 processed plays, **Zone** had the highest mean DDT Rate (23.8%; n=4,980) and **Man** the lowest (16.2%; n=2,183). This is descriptive geometry, not evidence that one coverage family is inherently worse or that assignment responsibility was known.

## Held-out baseline result

On 1,499 clean pass attempts from 25 held-out games, conventional geometry (B1) had log loss 0.5813; B1 plus DDT (B2) had 0.5810. The B1-minus-B2 log-loss difference was 0.00032 with a game-cluster bootstrap 95% interval of [-0.00055, 0.00106]. The interval crosses zero, so this fixed held-out split does not establish incremental completion-prediction value.

## Required interpretation

DDT identifies potential shared geometric stress before pass release. The data lacks individual coverage responsibility and all-route labels, so it cannot prove a coverage bust, a quarterback read, or a causal route-combination effect.
